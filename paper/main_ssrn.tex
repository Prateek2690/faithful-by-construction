\section{Reproducing the numbers}
\label{app:repro}
{\sloppy All results are computed from the saved model replies, and no API calls are
needed. \texttt{fetch\_fred\_universe.py} downloads the FRED series and builds the asset
universes (use \texttt{--end 2026-10-14} to obtain the data used here; the repository
gives its SHA-256 checksum). \texttt{run\_final\_study.py} builds the problems and queries
the models (seed 7 for the natural set; seed 11 with \texttt{--require\_slack} for the
slack-focused set). \texttt{reproduce.sh} then runs \texttt{make\_v5\_numbers.py},
\texttt{make\_v5\_example.py}, \texttt{robustness\_v5.py}, \texttt{computability\_v5.py},
\texttt{make\_v5\_table.py} and \texttt{make\_v5\_figures.py}, which produce every number,
the table and the figures. \texttt{audit\_numbers.py} recomputes the reported figures from
the result files and checks them against this PDF.\par}
